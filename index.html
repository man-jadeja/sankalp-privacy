Privacy Policy for Sankalp

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Last Updated: November 7, 2026

🛡️ Zero-Knowledge & 100% Client-Side Privacy: Sankalp does not require user registration or accounts. All data is processed in real time and stored strictly on your local device.

1. Overview

Sankalp ("we", "our", or "the app") is a digital wellbeing and mindful screen-time companion designed to help users curb addiction to short-form videos (such as YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels). Your privacy is our highest priority.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Sankalp collects ZERO personal information:

  • No name, email address, phone number, or credentials.
  • No reading or recording of chat messages, emails, or personal posts.
  • No location tracking (GPS or network).
  • No biometric, audio, or video data collection.

3. Accessibility Service API Disclosure

Sankalp uses Android’s AccessibilityService API strictly to serve its core wellbeing function:

  • Purpose: To detect when short-form video players (specifically YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels) are actively running in the foreground on your screen, count the number of videos viewed against your daily focus limit, and present a mindful pause screen when the quota is reached.
  • No Data Transmission: The Accessibility Service operates exclusively offline on your device. It does NOT read keystrokes, does NOT log private content, and does NOT send any data across the internet.
  • User Control: You can enable or revoke Accessibility permissions at any time via Android System Settings.

4. On-Device Storage & Local Data

The following configuration values are stored securely on your local device via Android Jetpack DataStore:

  • Your daily quota preferences.
  • Your local reel count for the current day.
  • Your chosen avatar companion and language preference (English, Hindi, Gujarati).
  • Your optional local safety PIN (hashed and stored locally).

This data never leaves your device and is permanently cleared if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Sankalp uses Google AdMob to deliver occasional optional rewarded ads (e.g., emergency limit reset) and standard non-intrusive banner ads. Google AdMob may collect standard device identifiers and advertising metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s Privacy

Sankalp is safe for all audiences, including children and families. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
